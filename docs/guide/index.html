Your records, from capture to backup

Capture documents, find saved information, protect private essentials, and keep a verified backup outside the app.

Deleting LifeAssist-AI removes its local records and captured files. Reinstalling alone does not bring them back. Create and verify an external backup before deleting the app. Backup files are not encrypted. All Private Secure Vault records and passwords are excluded; keep another safe copy of important vault information.

Capture a document and find it later

  1. On Home, select Capture Something and tap Continue.
  2. Choose Scan Document, Take Photo, Scan QR Code, Choose File, or Photo Library. Read the highlighted explanation, then Continue. Scanning requires a supported camera-capable device.
  3. For a multi-page scan, capture all pages and finish the scanner. For a file import, select the actual document in Files.
  4. Review Preview, then Continue to Describe & Organize. Enter a recognizable title, choose a category if useful, and tap Save capture.
  5. Check the Capture saved confirmation. Use View, Share, or Print, or open the saved item. Find it again in My Stuff or Search on this device.

When attaching to an existing item, open that item to find its document. Files selected from a cloud provider must be available to download. Canceling acquisition does not save a capture. Canceling QR-code capture returns to Capture.

Search and understand the matches

Type a saved title or a term from ordinary item information, attachment names, saved text, or captured codes. Search ignores letter case. One or two letters or digits match whole words; three or more characters allow partial matches. For example, CA does not match card, while car can match card. Matching words are highlighted. Expand attachment/code groups and tap the matching entry, or tap the item name/summary for its details.

Private Secure Vault records are excluded from Search. Open and unlock the Vault to find them. Records on another device do not appear automatically; there is no cloud sync.

Private Secure Vault

From Home, select Private Secure Vault and Continue. Unlock using Face ID, Touch ID where supported, or your device passcode. Choose one of the four sections: passwords, documents, notes/codes, or other secure records. Select a numbered record to see its available actions. Contents stay hidden until you explicitly open them.

Secure documents can be viewed, shared, or printed. Confirming Share or Print creates a copy outside the Vault. The Vault locks when you leave it or switch away. Expand Privacy, recording, and recovery details for the full explanation. Keep a separate safe copy: Vault information is excluded from app backups and ordinary Search, and does not sync between devices.

Compact Settings

Tap a numbered section to expand or collapse it. Use the fixed Expand all and Collapse all buttons to change the whole page. The main deletion/backup alert stays visible; expand Protect your saved information for its full details. Appearance and Color Theme let you choose a comfortable look.

1. Create and save a backup

  1. Open the Settings gear. Tap 2. Backup & Restore to expand it. Settings also has fixed Expand all and Collapse all controls.
  2. Tap Create backup / Save to Files. Wait for the system share sheet.
  3. Choose Save to Files. In Browse, choose iCloud Drive or On My iPhone, then a folder outside LifeAssist-AI’s own folder. Tap Save. Available destinations depend on your device and installed providers.
  4. Open the Files app and find the saved LifeAssist-Backup-….json file. Check its date and size. For cloud storage, allow the upload to finish. Keep the file unchanged.
  5. For added confidence, choose this file through Restore and check its backup date and item/attachment counts. You can then tap Cancel restore. This checks that the backup can be read; it does not perform a full restore.

iCloud Drive and On My iPhone backup and restore have been verified on an iPhone. An On My iPhone copy is still on that device: keep another copy off the device against loss or damage. Cloud storage here is a place for a manually saved file, not automatic app synchronization.

If you cancel the share sheet, no external backup is saved by that action. “Backup prepared” does not prove it reached your chosen destination. Verify the file before deleting anything. Make a new backup after important changes and keep an earlier verified copy until the new one is checked.

2. Restore records and attachments

  1. If the app already contains records, create a current backup first.
  2. Open Settings → 2. Backup & Restore → Choose backup to restore.
  3. In the Files picker, use Browse to open iCloud Drive or On My iPhone. Select the actual .json backup file, not its folder. A cloud file must finish downloading and be readable.
  4. Review the backup date and the counts of items, attachments, and reminders shown by the app. If they are unexpected, tap Cancel restore and choose another backup.
  5. Tap Restore missing items, then Restore in the confirmation. Keep the screen open until the completion message appears.
  6. Check the numbers added and skipped. Open My Stuff, choose All categories, and check both Active and Archived. Search by an item’s title. Open a restored item and View its attachments; a scanned multi-page PDF should retain its pages. Try Share or Print as needed.
  7. Open restored reminders in Item Details and recreate their notification alerts. Restoring reminder records does not schedule alerts automatically.

Restore adds missing items. Existing items with the same internal ID, including their attachments, stay unchanged; restore does not overwrite or repair them. Separately created items with identical titles can both appear. Archived items stay archived. Restore is not a way to restore Private Secure Vault information.

If restore reports a validation or file error, keep your original backup and current records. Do not edit the JSON or delete the app to troubleshoot. Contact support with the error text.

3. Google Drive: “content not available”

A Google Drive restore problem has been reported: the backup folder shows an item, but its content is unavailable when restoring. The cause has not been confirmed. Google Drive restore should not yet be treated as verified for this workflow.

  1. Open Google Drive and sign in to the account holding the backup. Check the internet connection and allow upload/download to complete.
  2. In the Files app, check Browse for Google Drive. If needed, use the Browse menu → Edit to enable the installed provider. See Apple’s instructions for third-party storage in Files.
  3. Find the actual LifeAssist-Backup-….json file. A folder count of “1 item” does not confirm the backup’s size, contents, or download availability. Do not select a folder, shortcut, or shared link.
  4. Try downloading or copying the backup into iCloud Drive or a folder under On My iPhone outside this app. Provider actions may be called Download, Send a copy, Share, or Save to Files. Confirm the copied JSON has a nonzero size.
  5. In LifeAssist-AI, choose the copied file with Choose backup to restore. Continue only after the expected backup summary appears.

Copying to a verified storage location is a suggested workaround; it has not yet been confirmed for the reported Google Drive file. Keep the original backup. If the copy is also unavailable or invalid, contact Support with the exact message, file size, provider, device/app versions, and whether the backup summary appeared. Do not email the backup or private documents.

4. Restore a backup stored on a Mac

The iPhone’s file picker does not browse the Mac’s local folders directly. First transfer the actual JSON file to a location available on the iPhone.

  1. iCloud Drive: copy the backup into iCloud Drive in Finder using the Apple Account available on your iPhone. Wait for it to upload, then locate and download it in the iPhone Files app.
  2. AirDrop: send the backup to the iPhone. If offered a destination, save it to Files in a folder outside LifeAssist-AI. Verify where it was saved before continuing.
  3. Open LifeAssist-AI → Settings → Backup & Restore → Choose backup to restore, select that JSON file, and follow the restore steps above. Receiving or opening a file alone does not restore it.

A Google Drive transfer can also be tried, but use the troubleshooting steps above if the provider cannot supply the file.

5. What a backup includes

  • Ordinary active and archived items, categories, notes, dates, saved QR/barcode values, extracted text and fields, and reminder records.
  • Saved attachment contents, including scanned PDFs, photos, and imported files—not just links to their old device locations.
  • Maximum backup size: 100 MB, including encoded attachment data.

Not included: all Private Secure Vault data and passwords, app appearance preferences, and scheduled notification alerts. Vault items are also excluded from ordinary Search. A shared PDF or photo by itself is not a records backup.

Anyone who can read the unencrypted backup can access its ordinary records and attachments. Store it privately, share it only with trusted destinations, and manage external copies separately. The developer cannot recover a backup you did not save.

To free storage while retaining app documents and data, iOS offers Offload App; Delete App removes local app data. See Apple’s storage guidance. An external backup remains important before device or app changes.

Support

Help and contact information for LifeAssist-AI on iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

For help with LifeAssist-AI, email angdembey.dra@gmail.com.

Include your app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Please remove personal information from screenshots and avoid sending private documents unless needed to explain your request.

Start with one thing

  1. On Home, select a choice, read its highlighted explanation, then tap Continue.
  2. In Add, give the item a title. Category, notes, captures, dates, and reminders are optional. Review and tap Save Item.
  3. Browse My Stuff, review Dashboard or Needs Attention, and use Search to find saved information.

Your selected choice is highlighted and checkmarked. You stay in control of when a workflow starts and when information is saved.

Frequently asked questions

Open a question for step-by-step guidance.

How do the Home choices work?

Choose Add Something, Capture Something, Voice Input, or Password Manager. A checkmark and highlighted row show the selection; the highlighted message explains it. Choosing a row does not start the workflow. Tap Continue when ready. Mouse or trackpad hover and keyboard focus also show hints.

How do I add an item in a category?

In My Stuff, select a category such as Vehicles, then tap the Add tab. That category is preselected in Add. An empty active category also offers Add an item with the same category preselected. You can change it before saving. All does not force a category; Uncategorized chooses no category. Other draft fields stay available.

Where are the other categories?

Swipe the category strip horizontally. More › appears when categories are hidden to the right; ‹ More appears when there are categories to the left. Tap either to scroll without changing your selection. Scroll vertically to see saved items and their information.

How do I return without saving?

On an existing item, Back returns to the screen you opened it from. Unsaved item edits prompt you to keep editing or discard them. On Add, Back returns to the previous tab without saving, and keeps your draft available. Saving a new item opens its details.

How do I capture a document, photo, file, or code?

From Home, choose Capture Something and Continue. Select Scan Document, Take Photo, Scan QR Code, Choose File, or Photo Library, read the selected method’s message, then Continue. Preview the result and choose where to save it. You can also start capture from Add or an existing item; those shortcuts may open the selected camera or picker directly. Canceling acquisition does not save a new attachment.

How does Voice Input work?

Choose Voice Input on Home and Continue, or use Voice Input beside Notes in Add. Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition access, start listening, then review the transcript. Use Text adds reviewed words to Notes; it does not save an item or execute spoken commands. Save Item when ready. On-device recognition depends on the device and language; typing remains available.

Where is Password Manager?

Open Home → Password Manager → Continue. Set a device passcode first, then unlock with Face ID, Touch ID where supported, or the device passcode. Passwords and PINs belong here, not in ordinary item notes. The area locks when you leave or switch away. Entries stay in this device’s protected Keychain; they do not sync, migrate through a backup, or have an app recovery service. Keep another safe copy and delete entries before uninstalling.

What do Home and Dashboard show?

Home shows Needs Attention and a compact Recent list. Dashboard provides an overview of items, categories, reminders, and dates. Tap a saved item to open its details. My Stuff lets you browse Active or Archived items and filter by category.

Can I change colors, appearance, or text size?

Open the Settings gear and choose System, Light, or Dark, then Classic, Soft Blue, Soft Green, Warm, or Lavender. Text follows your device’s text-size setting and layouts adapt on iPhone and iPad. Text-size changes should refresh without changing appearance. This website follows your browser/device appearance; it does not inherit the app’s selected palette.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. Core organization, local reminders, saved-file access, scanning, and text extraction work on your device. A cloud file provider, website, online sharing destination, or these support pages may require internet access.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check device Settings → Notifications → LifeAssist-AI and allow notifications. Also check Focus, notification delivery settings, and the reminder date and time. Reminder records remain visible in the app even if alerts are disabled. Alerts do not automatically complete reminders; review, complete, or reschedule them in the app.

Why does text extraction miss some text?

Use a clear, well-lit image with readable text. Check recognized text against the original. Imported PDFs need embedded text for PDF text extraction. An image-only imported PDF may return no text; scan the original or import its pages as images for image recognition.

Can I share or print documents?

Open a supported saved attachment or its extracted text and choose Share or Print. Sharing uses the destination you select. Printing opens the system print sheet and needs a compatible available printer. Not every file format supports preview or printing. Canceling leaves the saved attachment in place.

Are my records backed up or synchronized?

Version 1.0 has no app-managed cloud sync or complete backup and restore. Device backups may include ordinary app data depending on your settings. Share copies of important documents before deleting the app or changing devices; sharing individual files is not a full backup of items and reminders. Password Manager entries are device-only and do not transfer through backups.

How do I remove stored information?

You can remove attachments from item details. Archiving hides an item from active lists; it does not permanently erase it. Delete App removes ordinary local app data, while Offload App keeps documents and data. Originals in Files or Photos, shared copies, and backups are separate. Delete Password Manager entries in the protected area before uninstalling; Keychain entries can remain after app removal.

Does this version include cloud AI?

This release uses on-device text recognition, supported on-device speech recognition, and local search. It does not include live AI chat or cloud document analysis. Capturing a QR code or barcode does not automatically open a web link.

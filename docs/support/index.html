Getting started

  1. Open Add and save an Item with a title, category, notes, or important dates.
  2. Open the Item to attach files or photos, scan documents or codes, and add reminders.
  3. Use My Stuff to browse records, Today to see reminders, and Search to find Items and attachment text.

Common questions

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. Core organization, local reminders, saved-file access, scanning, and text extraction work on your device. Importing a file from a cloud storage provider, opening a web link, or using an online sharing destination may require internet access.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check Settings → Notifications → AI Life Assistant and allow notifications. Also check your Focus settings, notification delivery settings, and the reminder date and time. Reminders remain visible inside the app even if alerts are disabled.

How do I attach or scan a document?

Open an Item and choose the appropriate attachment action: Files, Photos, Camera, or document scanning. Allow camera or photo access when requested for the feature you choose. Canceling a picker or scan should return you to the Item.

Why does text extraction miss some text?

Use a clear, well-lit image with readable text. Recognition may make mistakes; check extracted text against the original. Imported PDFs need embedded text for PDF text extraction. An image-only imported PDF may return no text; scanning the original document or importing its pages as images allows image recognition.

Can I share or print my documents?

Open a supported attachment or its extracted text and choose Share or Print. Sharing uses the app or recipient you select. Printing uses the iOS print sheet and a compatible available printer. Canceling printing keeps the attachment.

Are my records backed up or synchronized?

Version 1.0 has no app-managed cloud synchronization or complete backup and restore. iOS device backups may include app data depending on your settings. Share copies of important documents before deleting the app or changing devices; sharing individual files is not a complete backup of Items and reminders.

How do I remove stored information?

You can remove attachments from Item details. Archiving an Item hides it from active lists and does not permanently erase it. To remove the app’s local data, use iOS Delete App, rather than Offload App. This may cause permanent loss of records. Copies you shared, originals in Files or Photos, and device backups are managed separately.

Does this version include cloud AI?

Version 1.0 includes on-device text recognition and local search. It does not include AI chat, cloud document analysis, or automatic AI suggestions.

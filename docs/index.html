No account required

Your records live in the app’s private storage. Core features work offline, without a sign-up step.

Choose, review, continue.

Home brings Add Something, Capture Something, Voice Input, and Password Manager into one simple selection group. A checkmark and highlighted explanation show what comes next. Tap Continue when ready.

Browse categories in My Stuff, carry a selected category into Add, and find your way back without losing your place. Expand matching groups in Search and select an attachment or code in Item Details to use one shared action area. Password Manager offers a numbered record list with creation dates for new entries and a shared action area for the checked record. Comfortable layouts, five color palettes, Light and Dark appearance, and larger text support make the app easier to read on iPhone and iPad.

A simple routine

Save it once. Find it when you need it.

  1. Create an Item

    Give a receipt, household record, or important document a place with a title and notes.

  2. Add the details

    Attach a document, scan a page or code, and set a reminder for an important date.

  3. Come back to it

    Browse My Stuff, check Dashboard, or search for information from saved documents.

Your information

Local by default.

Version 1.0 stores your records on your device and runs text recognition locally. It has no ads, account requirement, or live cloud AI service. Documents leave the app when you choose a sharing or printing destination.

There is no app-managed cloud sync or complete backup and restore in this release. Device backups depend on your iOS settings.

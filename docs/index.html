Your information

Private by design. Works offline.

Saved records stay on your device. LifeAssist does not automatically upload them to its servers or an AI provider. Use the Vault for passwords and sensitive documents: passwords use the protected iOS Keychain, and other Vault records and attachments are encrypted locally with a device-only key. Device authentication protects Vault access. Ordinary items do not have the Vault’s additional encryption. Vault contents are excluded from ordinary Search and Ask LifeAssist.

Core features work without an internet connection, including Airplane Mode with Wi-Fi turned off: create, edit and view local records, use the Vault, search ordinary saved items, scan documents, read supported document text on device, and use built-in Ask LifeAssist guidance and local lookups. Download cloud-only files before disconnecting. Web links, cloud file access and online sharing need a connection. Supported on-device voice recognition depends on the device and language; there is no cloud-recognition fallback.

Sharing, printing and exported backups can create copies outside the app; device backup settings may also send app data to iCloud or a computer. Protect your device and keep the encrypted Vault recovery key separate from its backup. No app can guarantee protection from identity theft.

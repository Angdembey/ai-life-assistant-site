Organize

Items and reminders

Save notes, categories, and important dates. Add local reminders and see an active-item category pie on Home and detailed reminders and dates on Dashboard.

Capture

Documents and photos

Attach files and photos, or scan multiple pages into a PDF. Keep related documents with the Item they belong to.

Find

Text extraction and search

Recognize text in images and scans, extract embedded PDF text, and search your Items, file names, and saved text. Matching words are highlighted; short searches match whole words.

Open supported images, text files, and PDFs. Share or print attachments and extracted text when you choose.

Keep local

No account required

Your records live in the app’s private storage. Core features work offline, without a sign-up step.

Choose, review, GO.

Home starts with Manage Private Vault Items selected. Under Create New Item Using, choose Camera Inputs & File Inputs, Voice Input Notes, or Form Input. A checkmark and visible explanation show what comes next. Tap GO when ready. The Home pie shows every active ordinary-item category, including one-item categories; tap Legends for colors and exact counts. The highlighted reminder alert separates Past and Upcoming counts and lists expired items separately.

Browse categories in My Stuff, carry a selected category into Add, and find your way back without losing your place. See highlighted matching words in Search and select an attachment or code in Item Details to use one shared action area. Private Secure Vault brings passwords, secure documents, notes/codes, and other secure records into separate compact sections. Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID where supported, or your device passcode. Vault items stay outside ordinary Search and ordinary item backups. Comfortable layouts, five color palettes, Light and Dark appearance, and larger text support make the app easier to read on iPhone and iPad.

Less clutter, one item at a time

Keep typed notes, reviewed Voice Input, and a photo, scan, file, or code together in one Add draft. The header Save creates one record. Choose an item type and its matching alphabetical categories; suggested notes help you fill only what matters.

Expand grouped details when needed. Summaries distinguish saved content from empty sections. Set date-linked or independent reminders, edit existing alerts, or use Cancel Reminder when an alert is no longer needed. Archive records for later, restore them with confirmation, or permanently delete selected archived items after making a verified backup.

Capture groups Camera Inputs and File Inputs. Hollow circles mark candidate methods and a checkmark marks the selected method. Its explanation and GO appear in a tinted card below the list, with general preview guidance beneath. The five methods let you scan paper pages into a PDF, take a photo, save a QR/barcode value, import a file, or reuse a library image. In Item Details, Add Code opens the scanner directly. Attachment cards keep View, Delete, and Extract Text together; Share and Print are inside View, and Full Text opens recognized text.

A simple routine

Save it once. Find it when you need it.

  1. Create an Item

    Give a receipt, household record, or important document a place with a title and notes.

  2. Add the details

    Attach a document, scan a page or code, and set a reminder for an important date.

  3. Come back to it

    Browse My Stuff, check Dashboard, or search for information from saved documents.

Your information

Local by default.

Version 1.0 stores your records on your device and runs text recognition locally. It has no ads, account requirement, or live cloud AI service. Documents leave the app when you choose a sharing or printing destination.

Use LifeAssistAI app Settings menu → Backup & Restore to save a manual backup of ordinary records and attachments outside the app. Restore ordinary items on the same or a new iPhone/iPad from the saved file. Existing same-ID records are kept. There is no automatic cloud sync. Private Secure Vault data is excluded from ordinary item backups. Inside the unlocked Vault, use its separate encrypted Backup & Restore with an owner-held recovery key for the same or another iPhone/iPad. Recreate reminder alerts after either restore. Read the backup and restore guide.

Privacy Policy

How AI Life Assistant version 1.0 handles your records, documents, and support messages.

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Who provides the app

AI Life Assistant is provided by Dhana Angdembey. This policy describes the iPhone app’s basic version 1.0 and this support website.

Local records, under your control

The app stores your Items, categories, notes, dates, reminders, attachments, extracted text, and captured QR/barcode values in its private storage on your device. It uses those records to organize your information, search it, display attachments, and provide reminders.

Version 1.0 does not automatically upload these records to a developer-operated server or an AI provider. No app account is required.

Scanning and text extraction

Camera capture, document scanning, QR/barcode recognition, image text recognition, and extraction of embedded PDF text run on the device. Results are stored locally with your Items or attachments. This release has no live cloud AI service.

Permissions

  • Camera: taking attachment photos, scanning documents, and capturing QR codes or barcodes.
  • Photos: selecting images you choose to attach.
  • Files: importing documents you choose through the system file picker. Your file provider may download a selected file from its own service.
  • Notifications: scheduling and showing reminder alerts locally. A reminder’s title or message may appear on your lock screen according to your iOS settings.

You can change permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission prevents the related feature from working but does not require you to stop using unrelated app features.

When information leaves your device

If you choose Share or Print, the selected content is sent to the destination app, recipient, service, or printer you choose. Their handling of that content is governed by their own terms and privacy practices. The app does not automatically send these copies to the developer.

If you choose to open a saved web link, your browser and the destination website may receive information about your visit. Capturing or storing a code does not automatically open its link.

Your iOS backup settings may cause app data to be included in an Apple or computer-managed device backup. The app does not provide its own cloud sync or complete backup/restore feature. Manage device backups separately through the relevant device or backup service.

Advertising, tracking, and diagnostics

Version 1.0 has no advertising, cross-app tracking, or app-integrated analytics or remote crash-reporting service. The developer does not sell your personal records or document contents.

Apple may process App Store information or device diagnostics under its own policies and your settings. Information you independently send for support is handled as described below.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain in the app until removed or until the app’s data is deleted. Removing an attachment removes its saved record and related extracted text; the app also attempts to remove the managed file. This does not delete the original in your Photos library or file provider, copies shared elsewhere, or copies in device backups.

Archiving an Item is not permanent deletion. Version 1.0 does not provide a complete in-app delete-all function. Using iOS Delete App normally removes the app’s local data; Offload App keeps its documents and data. Device backups and external copies must be managed separately. Save copies of important documents before deleting the app.

Because the developer has no remote copy of your local records, we cannot retrieve or erase those records on your device on your behalf.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any files or details you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. Email providers process those messages as part of delivering and storing email.

Support messages may be kept while resolving a request and afterward when needed for follow-up or legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive documents or redact them first when possible.

This website

These pages do not use advertising, analytics scripts, cookies set by this website, or contact forms. When you visit, the hosting provider may process standard connection information such as your IP address, browser details, and requested page to deliver and protect the website. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub’s handling of website traffic is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Website hosting is separate from the app’s local records.

Children’s privacy

The app does not require age information or registration and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through its local features. If a child has sent personal information to support, contact us to request its deletion.

Policy updates

We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s data practices change. Future features involving accounts, synchronization, analytics, or external AI processing would need updated disclosures and appropriate controls before being enabled.

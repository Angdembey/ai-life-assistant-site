Privacy Policy

How LifeAssist-AI version 1.1 handles your records, documents, and support messages.

Updated October 27, 2026 for version 1.1

Who provides the app

LifeAssist-AI is provided by Dhana Angdembey. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app’s version 1.1 and this support website.

Local records, under your control

The app stores your Items, categories, notes, dates, reminders, attachments, extracted text, and captured QR/barcode values in its private storage on your device. It uses those records to organize your information, search it, display attachments, and provide reminders.

LifeAssist-AI does not automatically upload these records to a developer-operated server or an AI provider. No app account is required.

Website appearance preference

This website stores your selected System, Light, or Dark appearance in your browser’s local storage. It is used only to remember the website appearance, is separate from app data, and can be removed by clearing this site’s browser data. It does not contain your saved items or passwords.

Private Secure Vault

The Vault separates passwords, secure documents, notes/codes, and other secure records from ordinary items. Access requires Face ID, Touch ID where supported, or the device passcode. The app does not receive biometric data or your device passcode. The Vault locks when you leave it or switch away, and its records are excluded from ordinary Search and ordinary Settings backups. Separate encrypted Vault backup and restore is available inside the unlocked Vault.

Passwords and the Vault’s encryption key are held in this device’s Keychain using device-only accessibility that requires a passcode. Other Vault records and document contents are encrypted in the app’s local storage. They do not sync automatically through the app. To move them to another device, use the separate encrypted Vault backup and its matching recovery key. Removing or resetting a device passcode, losing the device, or deleting app files can make Vault information unavailable. Keep another safe copy.

Secure documents offer Edit and Delete after authentication, plus View when an attachment exists. Share and Print are inside View. The app warns before sharing or printing a copy outside the Vault. Such copies are handled by the destination you choose and are no longer protected by the Vault. App-switcher previews are protected; screenshots, screen recording, and mirroring are not blocked on iPhone. Stop recording or mirroring before opening private information.

Delete private records from their Vault section to remove them. Keychain entries may survive uninstalling while encrypted files do not. Do not rely on app deletion to erase all Vault information or on reinstalling to recover it.

Encrypted Vault backups

Inside the unlocked Vault, you can explicitly export all four sections and secure document contents as an AES-256-GCM encrypted file. The app generates a random recovery key separately from the device-only storage key. The recovery key is shown for you to keep and confirm; it is not saved automatically or included in the backup file. If you explicitly choose Copy Recovery Key, iOS puts it on this device’s clipboard with a two-minute expiration and disables Universal Clipboard transfer for that copy. A destination where you paste it controls any saved copy. Backups are limited to 100 MB. No developer account or server is involved.

Export uses the destination you choose in the system share sheet. Anyone with both the file and recovery key could decrypt its contents. Keep them separately and safely. Restore on the same or another iPhone/iPad requires the matching key and destination-device authentication, previews counts, keeps existing IDs, and re-encrypts added records under the destination device’s protection. Restored reminder alerts stay off until you set them again. Support cannot recover a lost key. Ordinary unencrypted item backups still exclude all Vault data.

Scanning and text extraction

After a capture, choose Use in this item, then Review attachment contents → Read document details. For a saved item, select its attachment and open the same review. Compare View recognized text with View attachment. Select individual proposed fields or Select all suggestions, then Apply selected details, review the draft and Save. Notes can append or replace as offered. Apply alone does not save; reminders are set separately. OCR can misread text, dates and numbers, so verify against the original.

Selected fields kept by Save enter the encrypted Vault record. Raw recognized text and unselected suggestions remain transient; locking clears the review and dedicated temporary reading copies are removed. Vault documents are excluded from ordinary OCR storage, Search, Ask LifeAssist and ordinary backups. Keep private documents in the Vault rather than ordinary item Notes or attachments.

Camera capture, supported document scanning, QR/barcode recognition and supported text reading run on device. Ordinary saved attachment text belongs to ordinary item storage. Vault reading is explicit and authenticated. This release has no live cloud AI analysis.

Optional Voice Input

Voice Input uses supported on-device speech recognition and requires Microphone and Speech Recognition access. It may be unavailable for some device/language combinations and does not fall back to cloud recognition.

For Home Voice or Voice Input beside Notes, tap Start Voice Input, then speak. Speak now indicates active capture. Tap Stop, review or edit the transcript, then Use Text to place it in Notes. Nothing is saved until the item’s Save action. This full-screen notes flow is separate from inline voice in Ask LifeAssist. Voice does not interpret spoken commands or save records automatically.

Open Ask LifeAssist from Home. Type a question, tap a sample, or tap Voice to dictate directly into the question box. Speak now indicates microphone capture; tap Stop to keep the transcript for review. Voice adds words after any existing question. Review or edit the text, then tap Ask. Cancel Voice restores the pre-dictation question. The 500-character question limit still applies; voice never submits automatically. Leaving the screen stops its voice session.

Voice transcripts remain in the current draft until you choose the applicable Save or Ask action. Questions and answers are not retained as chat history.

Permissions

  • Camera: taking attachment photos, scanning documents, and capturing QR codes or barcodes.
  • Photos: selecting images you choose to attach.
  • Files: importing documents you choose through the system file picker. Your file provider may download a selected file from its own service.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: optional Voice Input for turning speech into reviewed item notes or Ask LifeAssist questions.
  • Notifications: scheduling and showing reminder alerts locally. A reminder’s title or message may appear on your lock screen according to your iOS settings.

You can change permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission prevents the related feature from working but does not require you to stop using unrelated app features.

When information leaves your device

If you choose Share or Print, the selected content is sent to the destination app, recipient, service, or printer you choose. Their handling of that content is governed by their own terms and privacy practices. The app does not automatically send these copies to the developer.

If you choose to open a saved web link, your browser and the destination website may receive information about your visit. Capturing or storing a code does not automatically open its link.

Your iOS backup settings may cause app data to be included in an Apple or computer-managed device backup. The app provides manual Backup & Restore for ordinary records and attachment contents. Exported JSON backups are not encrypted and exclude all Private Secure Vault data. Saving or sharing a backup sends that information to the location or recipient you choose, whose privacy practices apply. The app does not provide automatic cloud sync. Manage device backups separately through the relevant device or backup service. See the Backup & Restore guide.

Advertising, tracking, and diagnostics

LifeAssist-AI has no advertising, cross-app tracking, or app-integrated analytics or remote crash-reporting service. The developer does not sell your personal records or document contents.

Apple may process App Store information or device diagnostics under its own policies and your settings. Information you independently send for support is handled as described below.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain in the app until removed or until the app’s data is deleted. Removing an attachment removes its saved record and related extracted text; the app also attempts to remove the managed file. This does not delete the original in your Photos library or file provider, copies shared elsewhere, or copies in device backups.

Archiving an item retains it indefinitely on this device and makes its contents read only until restored. In My Stuff → Archived, Delete permanently removes a selected item and its related notes, attachments, saved codes, extracted text and suggestions, and reminders. There is no Undo. The app retries unfinished managed-file or notification cleanup. Original photos/files, exported backups, shared copies, and Vault records are unaffected. A backup made before deletion may restore missing ordinary records; restored reminder alerts must be recreated. LifeAssist-AI does not provide a complete in-app delete-all function. Using iOS Delete App normally removes the app’s local data; Offload App keeps its documents and data. Device backups and external copies must be managed separately. Create and verify a backup outside the app before deleting it. Reinstalling alone does not restore local records; Private Secure Vault data is excluded from the app’s backups.

Because the developer has no remote copy of your local records, we cannot retrieve or erase those records on your device on your behalf.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any files or details you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. Email providers process those messages as part of delivering and storing email.

Support messages may be kept while resolving a request and afterward when needed for follow-up or legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive documents or redact them first when possible.

This website

These pages do not use advertising, analytics scripts, cookies set by this website, or contact forms. When you visit, the hosting provider may process standard connection information such as your IP address, browser details, and requested page to deliver and protect the website. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub’s handling of website traffic is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Website hosting is separate from the app’s local records.

Children’s privacy

The app does not require age information or registration and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through its local features. If a child has sent personal information to support, contact us to request its deletion.

Policy updates

We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s data practices change. Future features involving accounts, synchronization, analytics, or external AI processing would need updated disclosures and appropriate controls before being enabled.

Contact

Ask LifeAssist

Privacy & Offline Questions appears first, followed by the collapsible Scan & OCR Questions group and More Questions. The three headings share bullet styling; sample questions use matching text rows. Ask offers built-in app guidance and local ordinary-record lookups, not general AI chat. It does not call an external AI provider or keep chat history. Vault contents and Vault reminders are excluded.

Typing or voice fills the question box locally. Ask must be tapped explicitly. Questions are not sent to an external AI provider. Result links and app actions open only when chosen.

Vault overview count

Home can show a generic total of records under Secure Documents. This excludes passwords, secure notes/codes, and other secure sections. An aggregate count is stored locally with device-only protection and refreshed after document changes; Home does not unlock or decrypt private records to draw the graph. Private document names and contents remain inside the Vault. Neutral secure-document reminder titles and times may appear in ordinary reminder overviews; selecting them opens the Vault and requires authentication.

Offline use

Core features work without an internet connection, including Airplane Mode with Wi-Fi turned off: create, edit and view local records, use the Vault, search ordinary saved items, scan documents, read supported document text on device, and use built-in Ask LifeAssist guidance and local lookups. Download cloud-only files before disconnecting. Web links, cloud file access and online sharing need a connection. Supported on-device voice recognition depends on the device and language; there is no cloud-recognition fallback.

## October 18, 2026 — build 10

The four pages reflect Home’s direct Vault/Capture/Voice/Form buttons, expanded welcome instructions, two-line chart heading, square Legends, zero-data solid chart placeholder, and shared Capture’s three Camera Inputs/two File Inputs icon buttons. iPhone offers dismissible press-and-hold help; iPad offers hover help within screen edges. The old Home/Capture selection and GO step is removed.

Ten WebP screenshots and three silent iPad previews were captured fresh from build 10. Native screens are preserved in full and scaled proportionally. Video cuts are edited for length; constant-frame-rate conversion holds existing frames during idle intervals. The document footage shows the Capture menu and an existing SPECIMEN PDF, not a completed scan/import/share/print. Vault examples are owner-confirmed fictional; passwords, secure values, authentication entry, recovery keys and backup-file contents are excluded. No backup/restore was executed for the media. Provenance and hashes: `docs/assets/media-manifest.json`.

The pages retain Coming soon wording and the canonical App Store link/QR until public release: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6818905932. App Review submission remains held. Publishing the site does not submit or release the app. Deployment verification is recorded in the app repository under `output/build10-media/publication-validation.json` after completion.

No analytics, tracking redirects, website forms, external fonts, or application account service are added. Website System/Light/Dark appearance is remembered in browser local storage, separately from app preferences. Ordinary item backups are manual, unencrypted and exclude the Vault; separate encrypted Vault recovery requires the matching owner-held key. Do not promise live AI or automatic cloud sync.
